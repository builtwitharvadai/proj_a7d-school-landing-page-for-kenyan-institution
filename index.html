About Our School

Since 2003, Mwangaza Academy has served families in Nakuru County with a commitment to academic excellence and holistic development.

Our Mission

To provide a safe, inclusive and inspiring environment where every learner grows in knowledge, character and confidence.

We pair strong academics with sports, the arts and community service, guided by dedicated teachers who know each learner by name.

Rooted in the Kenyan values of respect, unity and hard work, we prepare young people to serve their families, communities and nation.

Our Vision

To be a leading centre of learning in the Rift Valley, raising responsible, innovative and compassionate citizens for Kenya and the world.

Our History

Mwangaza Academy opened its doors in January 2003 with 42 pupils, three teachers and two classrooms in Nakuru's Milimani area. Founded by a group of parents and retired educators, the school takes its name from the Kiswahili word for “light”, reflecting their belief that education lights the way to opportunity.

Over two decades, we have grown into a day and boarding school of more than 850 learners from Pre-Primary to Form 4, with science laboratories, a library, a computer lab and sports fields. That growth has been built on strong partnerships with parents and the wider Nakuru community.

Our Programmes

From the early years to secondary school, we guide learners through every stage of their education.

Pre-Primary

PP1 – PP2

Play-based learning that builds early literacy, numeracy and social skills. Small classes and caring teachers help our youngest learners settle in with confidence.

Primary

Class 1 – Class 8

A strong foundation in English, Kiswahili, Mathematics, Science and Social Studies, alongside creative arts and physical education. Learners are well prepared for national examinations and the move to secondary school.

Secondary

Form 1 – Form 4

A broad KCSE curriculum covering sciences, humanities, languages and technical subjects. Career guidance and mentorship help every student plan their next step to university, college or work.
